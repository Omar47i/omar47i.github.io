Product direction / Prompt design / Review coordination

AI activity authoring for pedagogy teams.

I worked with pedagogy to turn generated drafts into learning activities the team could review and use.

Period
2025
Delivery
Internal release to pedagogy
My contribution
Squad scope, prompt iteration, expert review and delivery coordination

The problem

Activity creation required content writing and pedagogical judgment. Generated questions could become repetitive; a fluent passage could still be too simple for the intended learning task.

What I did

  • 01Drafted the squad’s framework and feature options, then reviewed the direction with the head of product.
  • 02Wrote and revised prompts using feedback from pedagogy specialists.
  • 03Coordinated walkthroughs, review status and the handover of finalised content to engineering.

A decision that changed the review

Pedagogy feedback showed that the instructions worked better than the generated passage. I proposed reviewing reusable instructions separately from passages and questions, then revised the passage prompt for another review. That gave us a more specific problem to work on than a single overall quality score.

How the team worked

I retained earlier prompt versions so the team could compare changes. When generated passages still risked falling short, I raised curated source material as a possible fallback. I also asked engineering to integrate finalised pieces while other content remained under review.

The initial generation engine was implemented and deployed by an engineering colleague. My contribution was the product direction, prompts, review process and coordination around delivery.

What was delivered

The internal release included reusable generators, guideline references and a how-to guide with a recording. The work supported content creation for English and Chinese programmes.
