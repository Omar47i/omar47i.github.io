Product design / AI-assisted implementation / Rollout

Curriculum planning and differentiated delivery.

A learning path affects more than the next activity. Staff need to plan it, understand it and correct it when the real schedule gets in the way.

Period
2026
Users
Pedagogy, teachers and customer success
My contribution
Curriculum design, planner and staff tools, implementation and rollout follow-through

The problem

A course needed to support different ability levels while preserving a shared structure. School schedules brought different document formats, revised plans and calendar exceptions. Staff also needed to see what a learner had completed and why particular practice had been assigned.

What I built

  • 01A document-driven course planner with a review step before saving.
  • 02Placement and differentiated-delivery capabilities within the existing learning platform.
  • 03Teacher and admin views for assignments, skill priorities and weekly progress.

Show the uncertainty before committing the plan

An ambiguous content match needed a visible choice. A schedule importer was only useful if staff could inspect the proposed plan, resolve uncertain matches and understand what would actually be assigned.

Keep class changes understandable

I worked through the distinction between an activity’s source settings, course settings and class overrides. Staff needed flexibility without a change to one class silently changing another’s setup.

That same consistency mattered in reporting: activity lists, access and progress views needed to follow the learner’s actual assignment.

Stay involved during rollout

I iterated with pedagogy and customer-success colleagues on school requirements, investigated a live placement problem and continued improving staff reporting after the initial implementation.

This extended an existing platform built by multiple contributors. My focus was the curriculum and planning workflow and the staff surfaces around it.

The evidence here is delivered capability and operational follow-through. It does not establish a measured learning gain or reduction in support volume.
