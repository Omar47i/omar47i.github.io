Prompt implementation / Pedagogy review / Design collaboration

Rewriting AI feedback for different learners.

Pedagogy flagged feedback that sounded mechanical, repeated itself or started too abruptly. I worked through the examples with them and turned that review into prompt changes.

Period
2025–2026
Delivery
Merged backend prompt changes
My contribution
Summary prompt updates, language-level examples, expert review and design feedback

What needed attention

The review covered tone, repetition and useful next steps, plus inconsistencies between an earlier assessment and a later feedback prompt. In related report work, teacher feedback also said the language was too technical for students and parents.

What I changed

  • 01Revised prompts to lead with a specific strength and explain improvement in simpler, more direct language.
  • 02Submitted changes across three lesson-summary formats and later added explicit output-language handling.
  • 03Drafted simple, moderate and advanced language examples for pedagogy review, and brought report feedback to the designer.

A friendlier explanation still has to agree with the assessment

I raised cases where a later feedback step contradicted an earlier mark. That made consistency part of the review alongside tone. Rewording a comment should not quietly become another grading decision.

What reached the product

My three summary-prompt updates were merged in October 2025, followed by output-language changes in January 2026. An engineering colleague integrated level-based guidance into the wider grading and summary workflows.

The prompt changes were my direct implementation. Wider adaptive-language routing was engineering work; the report’s visual design was a collaboration.

Later quality work, 2026

On another learning workflow, I traced feedback cut off after an abbreviated title to a sentence splitter used by a filter. I fixed that path and worked on summary checks that were rejecting useful advice. My update to the team distinguished the fixed truncation from generic filler that still needed work.

These are implementation and review results. They do not establish an increase in learning outcomes or that every feedback issue was resolved.
