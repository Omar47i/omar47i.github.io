Validation design / AI-assisted implementation / Recovery

Checking AI grading against the original marking rules.

Two totals can agree while the marks are attached to the wrong criteria or skills. I added checks that looked beyond the final number.

Period
2026
Context
An existing AI grading system
My contribution
Validation requirements, resolved grading inputs, score-boundary checks and recovery actions

The problem

Generated grading preparation needed to preserve the original marking policy. A converted rubric could match a total while missing an attainable score or assigning marks to the wrong skill.

What I implemented

  • 01Checks against original instructions, criterion marks and per-skill evidence.
  • 02Use of the resolved configuration intended for student grading, including the model answer and guidance.
  • 03Guards against automatic acceptance when required checks were incomplete or failed, plus recovery actions for staff.

Keep the expected judgment independent

A generated answer could not become correct simply because a repair changed its expected label. Validation needed original attainable scores, explicit coverage limits and cases such as paraphrases or missing and contradictory evidence.

What was delivered

The workflow exposed validation evidence and blocked automatic acceptance when required checks failed. Tests exercised those guards and the available recovery actions.

The grading engines were existing platform capabilities. My work covered validation and recovery around them.

Defined validation cases establish workflow behaviour. A provider benchmark would be needed to make a model-accuracy claim.
