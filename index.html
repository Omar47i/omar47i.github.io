How I work

The work happens between teams.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the workflow.

Ask pedagogy what good looks like, engineering what the system can support, and leadership which problem needs solving first.

02 / MAKE

Put an idea in front of people.

Write a prompt, build a prototype or map out the workflow so the team has something specific to react to.

03 / CHECK

Ask what could go wrong.

Bring examples back to the people who judge the work. A plausible answer or a passing total can still miss the learning goal.

04 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Stay through the rollout.

Coordinate the handover, explain the change and investigate what happens when real content and users reach the product.
